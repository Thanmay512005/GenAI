# Heritage background image attribution

The TravelMate page background uses **Stone Chariot at Vittala Temple Complex, Hampi**.

- Photographer: Prakruth Gowda
- Source: Wikimedia Commons — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stone_Chariot_at_Vittala_Temple_Complex,_Hampi.jpg
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Use: Displayed as a background image with a dark overlay for text readability. No edits to the photograph are made.

The image is loaded from Wikimedia Commons at runtime, so an internet connection is required for the photograph to appear. The rest of the application works without it.
