Q: What are some must-visit destinations in Karnataka?
A: Karnataka offers varied destinations including Mysuru for its palace and cultural heritage, Hampi for its historic ruins, Coorg (Kodagu) for its coffee-growing hills, and coastal destinations such as Gokarna and Udupi. Choose destinations based on your interests and available travel time.

Q: What is a good 3-day Karnataka itinerary?
A: For a short cultural trip, spend time in Mysuru exploring the palace and local heritage, then consider nearby attractions. For a nature-focused trip, plan a separate 3-day visit to Coorg with time for scenic viewpoints, coffee estates, and local experiences. Travel times depend on traffic and your starting point.

Q: When is the best time to visit Karnataka?
A: The most comfortable period for many destinations is generally October through March. Hill and coastal areas have different weather patterns, and monsoon months can bring heavy rain. Check local forecasts and attraction conditions before traveling.

Q: What should I know before visiting Hampi?
A: Hampi is known for its UNESCO-listed group of monuments and extensive historic landscape. Wear comfortable walking shoes, carry water, and allow sufficient time to explore. Check monument access and local guidance before your visit.

Q: What local food should I try in Karnataka?
A: Popular regional foods include Mysore masala dosa, bisi bele bath, ragi mudde, jolada rotti, Udupi-style dishes, and coastal seafood. Availability and ingredients vary by region; ask about spice levels and dietary needs.

Q: How can I plan a trip around Karnataka?
A: Start with your arrival city, trip duration, interests, and budget. Group nearby destinations into a practical route, account for road or rail travel time, and verify current transport schedules, accommodation, weather, and opening hours before departure.
