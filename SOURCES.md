# TravelMate Karnataka knowledge-base source notes

This is a curated educational travel guide, not an official government publication. District-level attraction selection and descriptions were cross-checked against these public references while preparing the November 2026 corpus:

1. Karnataka Tourism — Destinations and district highlights: https://karnatakatourism.org/en/destinations/
2. Government of India, Integrated Government Online Directory — Karnataka district directory: https://igod.gov.in/sg/KA/E042/organizations
3. Government of Karnataka, Bengaluru South District portal — district renaming and profile: https://bengalurusouth.nic.in/en/
4. Government of Karnataka, Karnataka Tourism Policy 2020–26 — focus tourism destinations: https://karnatakatourism.org/department/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Karnataka-Tourism-Policy-2020-26-v63.pdf
5. Government of Karnataka, Mysuru District portal — tourist places: https://mysore.nic.in/en/tourist-places/

## Editorial and safety notes

- The current 31-district list uses **Bengaluru South**. The district portal states that the erstwhile Ramanagara district was renamed Bengaluru South on 23 May 2025; Ramanagara remains the district headquarters and is still a destination within that district.
- Attraction lists are representative highlights, not exhaustive inventories.
- Sample itineraries are illustrative route-planning suggestions, not live navigation.
- Opening hours, ticket prices, permits, safari operations, road conditions, boat services, weather, sea conditions and public-transport schedules can change. The app tells travellers to verify time-sensitive details with official providers and local authorities.
- Wildlife, forest, trekking, waterfall and coastal activities must follow current authority guidance. No wildlife sighting or activity availability is guaranteed.
